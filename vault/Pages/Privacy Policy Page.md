# Privacy Policy Page

**In one line:** privacy.html is a static privacy policy (effective 30 August 2026) describing what Mindkraft collects, the two AI features run on Anthropic's Claude Haiku model, storage in Firestore asia-south1, what other users can see, sharing, controls, retention and contact.

## How it works
- Standalone HTML page linked from Settings › About & Legal (opens in a new tab); cached by the service worker as part of the app shell.
- Sections: who we are; information collected (account, content, social data, push, analytics); use; AI features; where data lives; what other users see; sharing (incl. Anthropic); controls; retention & security; children; changes; contact.
